IMPORTANT VALIDATION LIMITATION
-------------------------------
The saved prediction labels are internally consistent across
the two prediction files. However, 147 of 14,473 saved labels
disagree with the labels derived from their probability scores.

A separate inference run also produced different predictions.
Therefore, the reported classification metrics describe the
original saved labels and must not be treated as independently
reproduced until the prediction/probability discrepancy is
resolved.
