TECHNICAL HANDOFF: MULTILINGUAL FAKE-NEWS DETECTION
===================================================

MODEL
-----
XLM-RoBERTa-base, fine-tuned for binary FAKE/REAL classification.
Training time: approximately 66.32 minutes.
Training and saved model files are available in the research folder.

DATASET
-------
Total dataset: 144,711 articles.
English: 62,702.
Hindi: 67,177.
Gujarati: 14,832.

Train: 115,767.
Validation: 14,471.
Test: 14,473.

SAVED-PREDICTION EVALUATION
---------------------------
Accuracy: 99.53%.
Macro F1-score: 99.51%.
Weighted F1-score: 99.53%.

FAKE: Precision 0.9964, Recall 0.9920, F1 0.9942.
REAL: Precision 0.9946, Recall 0.9976, F1 0.9961.

Language-wise accuracy:
English: 99.3143%.
Gujarati: 99.7305%.
Hindi: 99.6874%.

These metrics describe the original saved predictions. They
must be treated as provisional until the discrepancy between
saved labels, probabilities, and independent inference is
resolved.

EXPLAINABILITY
--------------
SHAP output CSV and plot exist.
The current explanation values are extremely small and need
further validation before making strong interpretability claims.

CONFIDENCE
----------
Confidence calibration file exists.
Most examples fall in the 0.9-1.0 confidence bin.
Calibration is preliminary because of the unresolved
prediction/probability inconsistency.

TRUSTED-SOURCE RETRIEVAL
------------------------
A preliminary Wikipedia retrieval prototype exists.
Retrieval is not equivalent to fact verification. The current
prototype does not establish the truth of news claims.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
1. Resolve the saved-label/probability mismatch.
2. Reproduce final metrics from one consistent inference run.
3. Validate SHAP explanations before strong claims.
4. Improve and evaluate trusted-source verification.
5. Do not describe the retrieval prototype as a completed
   fact-checking system.
